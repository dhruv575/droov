Be Audacious

How I pivoted from @morganstanley and @janestreetgroup by forcing my way into working at @Kalshi, @Polymarket, @mtslive, and @a16z in the span of 8 months.

Dhruv Gupta

Coming into freshman year at Penn, I didn't really know that jobs outside of FAANG existed. I heard about consulting for the first time while striding along Locust Walk when I committed the unfortunate mistake of making eye contact with someone manning the GRC booth. The allure of banks and funds was foreign to me.

I secured my sophomore summer quant internship at Morgan Stanley before I had secured something for my freshman summer, and also largely by accident. While spam applying to summer 2024 roles on Handshake, I ended up submitting something for a summer 2025 role as well, went through 3 rounds of interviews, and got the job.

It was in that job that I realized I fucking hate corporate. When I wanted an LLM api key in my last couple of weeks to run a few experiments, I was told "if you'd requested that key 2 months before your internship, you may have gotten it by the time you started fulltime." Work was slow, expectations were low, and it felt like people had perfected the theater of work more than they had the work itself.

I started making Tiktoks in my senior year of high school to impress a girl (why did I think this would work, who knows). After having a few early Tiktoks go relatively viral (1, 2, 3) I made it a habit to post content somewhat regulalry, mostly over the summers. Over time, I became what some call a "niche micro-celebrity" focused mostly around engineering/quant content.

Prediction markets had always interested me from an economic utility standpoint, and when I got the above dm from @salmanso_, I felt like a lucky lucky boy. I made a few videos, none too inspiring, before realizing that I would much prefer working on the actual product. Long story short, there was no place for me to do that at Kalshi, so I sent the rather inflammatory email below to the only Penn alum who worked at Polymarket.

Though I did get an initial response from @wasabiboatresearch, I ended up getting ghosted after. So I spent 4 hours feverishly coding up the 2 rather rudimentary projects below, which piqued Will's interest enough to commission me for a piece of software he wanted for https://news.polymarket.com, which eventually turned into me helping build tooling around https://polymarket.com/polygraph and developing https://thespread.news, writing a couple of articles (1, 2, 3) as well.

While working at Polymarket, I also accidentally almost started 2 startups in the space with the brilliant @no__________end (co-founder of both Augur and Gnosis) and a statistics professor at Wharton who was one of the world's foremost experts in markov chains + a lover of prediction markets as a tool for teaching probability.

While neither of these ended up panning out, I learned an incredible amount about fundraising, people, and a couple of niche technologies (if you would like a sneak peek of something I've been working on in relation to this, check this out).

Around the time the second of these ventures was closing its pre-seed round and looking for full commitment from me, I had realized that I would rather be building in a space I truly loved, media. Luckily for me, @a16z was opening up their second cohort of New Media Fellows at the same time, spearheaded by then partner @liangsays.

We met, we stayed in touch, and when Brent left Andreesen Horowitz to found MTS, he wanted someone to build things similar to what I'd been working on at Polymarket. My contract there was coming to an end, I loved the news and especially the show The Newsroom, and I thought the opportunity to larp as Dev Patel's tech-forward journalist character from the show sounded extremely appealing.

Brent and I spoke about me joining MTS for the first time on the morning of their 3rd day live streaming. By the end of that day, I had negotiated with all my professors to take online finals, packed up all my bags, said an early goodbye to all my graduating senior friends, and hopped on a flight to a month-long airbnb in the Tenderloin.

While I was initially just scoped to build https://www.mts.now/situations, it became clear to me early on that MTS had the chance to fill a critical gap in the discourse. After we had some early hits covering the Musk v. Altman trial, I felt motivated to keep on building.

Making a daily high-quality explainer in the times of Opus 5.5 and Astra feels trivial, but April 2026 was a totally different world. I had so much fun teaching the timeline about data center buildouts, UFOs, startup hiring, and so much more.

People fucking hated us in those early days. Being in those war rooms with Brent and @netcapgirl 12 hours a day 7 days a week was one of the most worthwhile experiences of my life, and I still regret leaving them to go through with my summer intermship at Jane Street.

MTS · @MTSlive · May 7, 2026

We built a way to explore everything that's been entered into evidence for the Musk v. Altman trial. Greg's journal. Texts between Elon and Sam. OpenAI's LP agreement and more. All at our latest drop: evidence.mts.now

Amongst all the noise, only we knew who we were, what we were building, and why we were building it. Keeping our heads down and just trying to be 10% better every day has clearly paid off for the team, and I'm so bullish on everything that is still to come for them.

I only had 6 classes left after this summer, so the reasonable choices were load them into one semester and graduate in December or take a light workload in the Fall while working. I had begun chatting with @eriktorenberg during my time at MTS and over the summer he pitched me on a vision of what would eventually solidify into @cosign.

There's likely no product in the world I feel more morally aligned with than Cosign. A friend reminded me recently that for almost an year now, I had been running my own "list" of eligible talent that I'd been sending to founders and VCs with the hopes of getting underclassmen I mentored internships in the startup space. I hope Cosign can become the place for friends and mentors to highlight those hustlers, fanatics, warriors, and psycopaths that they come across.

Getting here has been quite the journey. I'm excited to keep on improving Cosign 10% a day. It's time to build!
